# addChildAbuse
Child Safety & CSAE (Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation) Policy
At Rajesh Dada App, we are fully committed to ensuring a safe and respectful environment for all users. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including any content, communication, or behavior that promotes or enables such activity.
Our Commitment
- We do not allow any form of content or behavior that involves or promotes CSAE.
- We actively monitor and take prompt action to detect and remove any such material.
- We collaborate with law enforcement and relevant authorities to report and respond to CSAE incidents.
Reporting and Moderation
- Users can report any suspicious or abusive content through our in-app reporting feature or by contacting us directly at contact@exaltasoft.in
- All reports are reviewed promptly by our moderation team.
- Confirmed violations will result in immediate removal of content and suspension or banning of the responsible accounts.
Law Enforcement Cooperation
We cooperate fully with law enforcement agencies and may report content that violates this policy to organizations such as:
- The National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)
- Local and international law enforcement authorities
Educational Resources
We encourage parents, guardians, and users to learn more about online child safety through trusted resources:
- https://www.missingkids.org
- https://www.childhelplineinternational.org
Contact Us
If you become aware of any content or behavior that may involve CSAE on our platform, please report it immediately to:
